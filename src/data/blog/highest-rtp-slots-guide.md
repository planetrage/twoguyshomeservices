## Introduction

When it comes to online slots, players often seek the best odds to maximize their winnings. One crucial metric to consider is the Return to Player (RTP) percentage. RTP represents the expected return a player can receive over time and is a key indicator of a slot's potential profitability.

## Key Concepts

RTP is expressed as a percentage, calculated over a long period and across many players. For instance, a slot with an RTP of 96% indicates that, theoretically, for every $100 wagered, $96 would be returned to the players over time. Another important concept is volatility, which measures a slot's risk level.

## Summary

Understanding RTP is crucial for anyone interested in playing online slots. Higher RTP slots generally provide better odds, but remember that luck plays a significant role in individual sessions.
