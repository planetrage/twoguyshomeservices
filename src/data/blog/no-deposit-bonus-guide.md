## Introduction
The world of online gambling is filled with enticing offers and promotions, one of the most popular being the no deposit bonus. This type of bonus allows players to explore various games without depositing their own money upfront.

## Key Concepts
A no deposit bonus is typically offered to new players upon registration. It may come in various forms such as free spins, bonus cash, or bonus credits. Wagering requirements determine how many times you must wager the bonus amount before withdrawing winnings.
