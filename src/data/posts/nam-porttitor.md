singlePostTitle: Volatility vs. Variance — What Players Get Wrong
singlePostDesc: Most players confuse volatility with variance, or think high volatility simply means "bigger wins." The reality is more nuanced. Volatility describes the distribution of payouts — how frequently wins occur and how large they are relative to the bet. A slot can have high volatility with a modest max win if the base game hits are extremely rare. We analyze payout distributions from 50 popular slots across all three volatility tiers and show you exactly what to expect from each.
